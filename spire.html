Radiant Spire Planner

On Sunday you fight with 1, 2 or 3 squads against the same number of enemy squads. Each of your squads first meets the enemy squad in the same slot, then the survivors keep fighting with the troops they have left. This planner splits your troops between your squads and finds the ratios that win most often.

How to use it (2 minutes)
  1. Pick how many squads you have in the game's Squad Config screen.
  2. Your troops: type your troop counts per level from Edit Formation (Elite VIII = level 8, Heroic VI = level 6).
  3. Stats: copy each squad's bonus % and the enemy's from a Radiant Spire battle report (Bonus Details: left column is you, right column is the enemy).
  4. Lost attempts (recommended): after a loss, enter the ratios you used and the troops left after each round. The planner uses this to measure the hidden hero strength.
  5. Tap Plan my Sunday and set your squads exactly as the plan says.

How it plans

It simulates the whole Sunday fight, round by round, for every combination of ratios at 5% steps, refines the best area at 1% steps, then replays the top plans hundreds of times with the random cavalry and archer procs. Every plan gets the same random rolls, so the ranking compares plans, not luck.

How it calibrates

It finds one strength factor for each of your squads after the first, and one for each enemy squad, so that the model reproduces the troops left after every round of your lost attempts. On stage 8-10 it reproduced six real rounds to within 1.5%, and its plan cleared the stage.

Limits

  • Level 7 and 8 base stats are estimated between the known level 6 and 9 values.
  • A calibration only holds for the same heroes and the same stage.
  • Pairing after round 1 follows what battle reports show (first survivors meet first). Kingshot hasn't documented it.
  • Squad 3 unlocks after clearing Stage 25-10. When Squad 2 unlocks isn't documented.
  • Community reverse-engineered formula, not official.
