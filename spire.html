Radiant Spire Planner

Sunday is two of your squads against two enemy squads, fought in rounds: Squad 1 vs enemy 1, Squad 2 vs enemy 2, then the survivors fight on. This planner splits your troops between both squads and finds the pair of ratios that wins most often. Hero skills can't be read from the game, so it learns their effect from the round results of attempts you already lost.

Calibration

How it plans

It simulates the full Sunday fight (rounds 1, 2 and the survivors' rounds) for every pair of ratios at 5% steps, refines the best area at 1% steps, then replays the top contenders hundreds of times with the random cavalry and archer procs. Each contender gets the same random rolls, so the ranking compares plans, not luck.

How it calibrates

The troop formula can't see hero skills. Instead it finds three strength factors (your Squad 2 relative to Squad 1, and each enemy squad) that best reproduce the troops left after every round of your lost attempts. On stage 8-10 this reproduced six real rounds to within about 3%.

Limits

  • Level 7 and 8 base stats are estimated between the known level 6 and level 9 values.
  • The calibration only holds for the same heroes and the same stage.
  • Community reverse-engineered formula, not official.
