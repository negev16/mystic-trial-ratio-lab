How a fight is calculated

Mon–Sat zones give both sides the same level-10 troops, so only your zone's stats change the result. Each round both sides hit at the same time:

attack = base_atk × (1+Atk%) × 10 × (1+Leth%) / 100 defense = 10 × (1+Def%) × base_hp × (1+HP%) / 100 army = √(squad troops × smaller army total) kills = army × attack / enemy defense × counter / 100

Every squad hits the first enemy line still standing: infantry, then cavalry, then archers. A counter hit (Inf→Cav, Cav→Arc, Arc→Inf) does +10%. Cavalry skip to the archers 20% of rounds, archers fire twice 10% of rounds. The fight runs until one side has no troops.

Why 1% can matter

The result hinges on which line collapses first and in which round. Moving 1% can make your infantry last one round longer, which lets your archers fire one more volley. That is why the map has sharp edges instead of a smooth hill, and why the lab tests every 1% step.

Replays use the same random rolls for every contender, so the ranking compares ratios, not luck.

Limits

  • The formula is the community's reverse-engineering of the shared State of Survival engine, checked against Kingshot battle reports. It is not official.
  • Hero skills and pet skills are not simulated. That matters in the Coliseum, the Radiant Spire and the Forest of Life.
  • Garbage in, garbage out: the answer is only as good as the stats you copy in.
